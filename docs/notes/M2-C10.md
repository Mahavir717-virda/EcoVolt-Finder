## 1. Objectives & Scope

Build the analytical and reporting layer for all three primary user personas:
1. **Driver (`GET /impact/me`):**
   - Personal lifetime metrics: total sessions completed, total energy delivered (kWh), total money spent (₹), ₹ saved vs standard DISCOM sticker price, total $CO_2$ avoided (kg), and weighted average renewable % achieved across sessions.
2. **Station Manager (`GET /analytics/station/:id`):**
   - Station-level analytics: total completed/active sessions, total revenue (₹), total energy delivered (kWh), average renewable %, hourly utilization breakdown, and **Demand-Charge Risk Signal** (current peak concurrent load kW vs station threshold kW).
   - **Ownership Scoping (Edge Case #22):** A manager can only access analytics for stations belonging to their operator organization.
3. **Admin (`GET /analytics/network`):**
   - Network-wide macro metrics: aggregate active & completed sessions, total network energy (kWh), total network revenue (₹), grid load share by provider/zone, overall green energy share (%), and total charging sessions shifted into optimal renewable windows.
   - **Role Guard:** Strictly restricted to users with `role: admin`.

## 2. Mathematical Aggregations & Formulas

### 1. Driver Impact (`GET /impact/me`)
$$\text{totalSessions} = \text{COUNT}(\text{sessions where status = 'completed'})$$
$$\text{totalKwh} = \sum \text{energyKwh}$$
$$\text{totalSpent} = \sum \text{cost}$$
$$\text{savedVsSticker} = \sum (\text{energyKwh} \times \text{baseTariff}) - \text{totalSpent}$$
$$\text{co2AvoidedKg} = \sum \text{co2AvoidedKg}$$
$$\text{avgRenewablePct} = \frac{\sum (\text{avgRenewablePct} \times \text{energyKwh})}{\sum \text{energyKwh}} \quad (\text{or simple mean if } \sum \text{energyKwh} = 0)$$

### 2. Manager Station Analytics & Demand-Charge Risk (`GET /analytics/station/:id`)
$$\text{peakThresholdKw} = \sum (\text{connector.powerKw} \times \text{connector.totalCount}) \times 0.80$$
$$\text{activeLoadKw} = \sum_{\text{active sessions}} \text{connector.powerKw}$$
$$\text{demandChargeRisk} = \begin{cases} \text{"high"} & \text{if } \text{activeLoadKw} \ge \text{peakThresholdKw} \\ \text{"medium"} & \text{if } \text{activeLoadKw} \ge 0.6 \times \text{peakThresholdKw} \\ \text{"low"} & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

## 4. Key Edge Cases Addressed

- **Edge Case #16 (Demand-Charge Risk):** Computes live concurrent active load vs station grid connection limit to warn managers before triggering costly grid penalty surcharges.
- **Edge Case #22 (Manager Scoping):** Throws `403 Forbidden` if a manager requests analytics for another manager's station.
- **Zero-Division Protection:** Safe fallbacks when a user/station has 0 completed sessions.
