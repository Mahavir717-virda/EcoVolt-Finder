# M3-C10: Smart-Charge Optimizer

## Objective
Provide a tangible, economic, and environmental reason for a driver to *wait* to charge. 
By sliding their charging session to a cleaner/cheaper window in the grid forecast, we reduce peak load and carbon emissions.

## Inputs
- `energyNeededKwh`: How much energy to charge.
- `chargeRateKw`: Speed of the charger.
- `deadlineLocal`: By when the car must be fully charged and ready.
- `urgent`: If true, bypass all optimization and start immediately.
- `tariff`: The baseline cost.

## The Logic
1. **Urgent Short-Circuit**: If `urgent == true`, schedule charging to start at `now()`. `isImmediate = true`. Savings = 0.
2. **Duration Calculation**: `durationHours = energyNeededKwh / chargeRateKw`. 
3. **Infeasibility Fallback**: If the `durationHours` strictly exceeds the time until `deadlineLocal`, we start charging immediately and add a `note`: "Cannot complete full charge by deadline; started immediately to maximize range."
4. **Forecast Fetch**: Retrieve the grid forecast for `zoneId` (via M3-C5 model) spanning the time until the deadline.
5. **Window Search (Sliding Window)**:
   - Identify all contiguous time slots (stepped every 30 mins) that can fit `durationHours` *and* complete strictly before `deadlineLocal`.
   - Calculate the expected `renewablePct` for each candidate window by sampling the forecast at the window's midpoint.
   - For cost optimization, calculate the savings. If the optimal window improves the renewable percentage significantly compared to charging "now", we calculate simulated `expectedSavings` (e.g. ₹2/kWh saved by shifting off-peak).
6. **Concurrency Hint**: (Optional / Stretch) To avoid the "herd effect" where 100 EVs are told to charge exactly at 2 PM, we can add a small random jitter to `startLocal` or apply a load-balancing penalty based on the `stationId`.

## Payoff Presentation
The user is presented with the precise `startLocal` and `endLocal` times, a computed `expectedSavings` in ₹, the `expectedRenewablePct`, and a plain-English `note` detailing why this window was chosen or why optimization was skipped.
