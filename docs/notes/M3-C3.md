# M3-C3: Renewable Classification & Zone Mapping

## Taxonomy
- **RENEWABLE** = {solar, wind, hydro, biomass, geothermal}
- **CARBON_FREE** = RENEWABLE ∪ {nuclear}
- **FOSSIL** = {coal, gas, oil, diesel}

## Hydro Convention
Hydro counts as a renewable energy source by default. However, this is configurable via a single flag (`HYDRO_IS_RENEWABLE`) to easily flip its classification if regulatory definitions change.

## Unknown / Other Handling
Any generation labeled as `unknown` or `other` is strictly excluded from the numerators and denominators when calculating percentages. We compute an `unclassifiedPct` to flag the unknown proportion of the grid mix, but we never guess or assign it to either clean or dirty categories.

## Metrics
- `renewable_percentage(mix)`: (Sum of RENEWABLE) / (Sum of all known generation) * 100
- `carbon_free_percentage(mix)`: (Sum of CARBON_FREE) / (Sum of all known generation) * 100
- `band(pct)`:
  - `>= 80`: `very_high`
  - `>= 65`: `high`
  - `>= 50`: `medium`
  - `>= 20`: `low`
  - `< 20`: `very_low`
