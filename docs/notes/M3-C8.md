# M3-C8: Travel-Cost & Range Model

## Core Math

### 1. Travel Energy (kWh)
To reach a station, a vehicle consumes energy.
`Travel Energy (kWh) = distanceKm × efficiency (Wh/km) / 1000`
- **Bike**: Typically ~20-30 Wh/km.
- **Car**: Typically ~150-200 Wh/km.
Because cars are heavier, driving 10km costs significantly more energy for a car than a bike.

### 2. Travel Cost (₹)
Energy isn't free. The travel cost is the opportunity cost of the energy used to reach the station, based on a reference rate (e.g., home charging tariff).
`Travel Cost (₹) = Travel Energy (kWh) × Reference Rate (₹/kWh)`

### 3. Reachability (Range Check)
We must ensure the vehicle has enough battery to reach the station. We also apply a safety factor (e.g., 1.2x) to account for traffic, elevation, or AC usage.
`Current Capacity (kWh) = (currentChargePct / 100) × batteryKwh`
`Max Range (km) = (Current Capacity (kWh) × 1000) / efficiency (Wh/km)`
The station is `reachable` if:
`Max Range (km) ≥ distanceKm × safetyFactor`

*Note on return trips*: This model only checks reachability *to* the station. It is assumed the vehicle will charge at the destination to cover the return trip or onward journey.
